# TPMForge — Vision, Mission & Product Philosophy

## Tagline
**Forge the Next Generation of Technical Program Managers**

---

## Vision
Build the world's most comprehensive AI-powered Career Operating System for Technical Program Managers.

The platform helps professionals transition into TPM roles, continuously improve competencies, prepare for interviews, earn certifications, receive AI-powered coaching, and become world-class leaders.

---

## Mission
Enable every engineer, QA, Product Manager, Engineering Manager, Business Analyst, Consultant, and aspiring leader to become an exceptional Technical Program Manager through AI-powered personalized learning.

---

## North Star
Become the global standard for TPM learning, competency assessment, interview preparation, certification, and enterprise capability development.

**Metric:** % of active users who achieve a verified competency improvement (measured by assessment delta) within 90 days.

---

## Product Principles

1. **Competency-First, Not Course-First** — Measure what you can *do*, not what you *completed*.
2. **Graph-Driven, Not AI-Hallucinated** — Every question, roadmap step, and recommendation traces to a graph node.
3. **Assessment Over Consumption** — Rubric-scored evidence beats video watch-time.
4. **Single Source of Truth** — One graph, one schema, one scoring model. No duplicate definitions.
5. **Free-First Architecture** — $0/month fixed cost; free LLM tiers; pay only when earning.
6. **Schema-Driven Extensibility** — Adding a new career path (EM, PM, Staff Eng) = new graph data, not new code.
7. **Privacy by Default** — User data stays in their Supabase project; free-model logging acknowledged and consented.
